Mobile fixes — before and after

The fourteen fixes from the mobile audit, as built. Each “Today” picture is the site before (live, 2026-11-08), each “Branch” picture the new code, on the same emulated phones. Tap a picture to see it full size.

Status: picked B on all 14 by the owner, built on the branch mobile-audit and checked on emulated phones and desktop, then merged into main and pushed live on 2026-11-08. Live site: karl0s.github.io/digital-vault.

The research behind it: the audit report (mobile-audit.html), docs/ux-research/06-mobile-audit.md (findings, scores, what was built) and 07-mobile-case-study.md (the brief, the plan, the decisions and the timeline).

R1An artist from the A–Z list opens at the top

Tapping Pearl Jam landed on the bottom of its shows. Now the page jumps to the top. Also on desktop.

R2 · R4Two columns, results right under the bar

Pearl Jam: 1 card a screen, 10.8 screens → 6 a screen, 2.9 screens. The big title, “Found 24” and Clear search are gone on phones; All artists stays.

R4Results above the keyboard

“brasil” with the keyboard up: no shows visible → 4. The row under the search steps aside while typing; the Search key closes the keyboard.

R3Initials beside other words

“ratm swu” found nothing → the 2 SWU shows. Also “fnm bizarre 97”, “qotsa reading”, “30stm rar”. Also on desktop.

R6A letter index on the A–Z list

Tap or drag the letters at the right edge. All artists (and Back) return to the exact place you left the list.

R7A slim filter bar

Four filters on: the bar was 235px with chips, 1 show on screen → 121px, 6 shows. One row: Filters (4), count, list/cards, sort.

R8Filters in a sheet

Every filter in one sheet, 44px chips with live counts, “Show 34 shows” at the foot. Drag down, tap outside or × to close.

R10 · R13A shorter masthead, and New one tap away

First screen: 1 show → 4. “● New · 64” beside Filters opens the 64 new shows.

R10No masthead with the phone sideways

R9Copies compared on a phone

Each copy stacked; what differs lit in amber, what is shared under “Same”.

R11The docked show header is one line

150px → 56px while you read the setlist or info file.

R1244px touch targets

Close, the viewer’s Back / arrows / dots, Technical details, A–Z rows, the search field’s ×, every bar and sheet control. Share and Open full size keep their look with a bigger touch area.

DesktopDesktop looks the same

Pixel-identical to the live site on an artist page and on filtered results at 1440×900. What does change there: R1, R3, Back stepping through the site (R5), All artists returning to your place (R6), and the first four stills loading first (R14).
