Recorded in a headless iPhone-size browser (390×844) with real touch events, at real speed.

Swipe between screenshots on a phone

The show drawer's screenshot viewer, touch screens only. Not pushed.

In the recording, in order

  1. Drag left past halfway and let go: the next screenshot.
  2. A short quick flick: the next one. Momentum counts, so a flick is enough.
  3. Drag right, change your mind and go back: it stays put.
  4. Two flicks right: back to the first.
  5. Drag right past the first screenshot: it resists, then springs back. No wrap-around.

How it behaves

  • The picture stays under your finger the whole way; after you let go it carries on at your finger's speed and settles without a bounce.
  • "Screenshot 2 of 4" and the dots change as you let go, not after the slide.
  • Touch a picture while it is still sliding and it stops under your finger.
  • Up-and-down movement and pinch-zoom work as before; zoomed in, a finger pans the page.
  • A swipe never closes the viewer; a tap on the dark area still does.
  • The arrows and dots work as before (they still wrap around). On a computer with a mouse nothing has changed.

Try it on your phone

In the project folder run npm run dev -- --host, open the "Network" address it prints on your phone (same Wi-Fi), open a show and tap a screenshot.
